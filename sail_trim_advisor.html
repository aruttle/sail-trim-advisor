Sail Trim Advisor

Enter the conditions, how the boat feels, and your current sail setup. The app suggests sensible trim changes in priority order, including mainsheet, traveller, headsail trim, outhaul, backstay, and kicker/vang.

Conditions

This first version uses simple rule-based logic, which is good news: transparent, editable, and free.

Recommendations

Pick the conditions and click Get trim advice. The result panel will suggest what to change first, why, and what to watch next.
